BANDIT KINGS OF ANCIENT CHINA - REFERENCE DATA
===============================================

GAME BACKGROUND
---------------
Bandit Kings of Ancient China (水滸伝・天命の誓い Suikoden: Tenmei no Chikai)
is based on the Chinese classical novel "Water Margin" (Shui Hu Zhuan).

The game features 108 generals based on the 108 Stars of Destiny from the novel.

WEAPON TYPES
------------
0x00 - Unarmed
0x01 - Sword
0x02 - Spear
0x03 - Axe
0x04 - Staff
0x05 - Bow
0x06 - Special Weapon
0x07 - Legendary Weapon

SPECIAL ABILITIES
-----------------
0 - None
1 - Tactician (+20% strategy success)
2 - Warrior (+20% combat damage)
3 - Diplomat (+30% negotiation)
4 - Spy (reveals enemy info)
5 - Merchant (+20% trade income)
6 - Bandit Leader (recruiting bonus)
7 - Strategist (advanced tactics)

CHARACTER CLASSES
-----------------
0 - Foot Soldier
1 - Cavalry
2 - Archer
3 - Commander
4 - Strategist
5 - Special Class

TERRAIN TYPES
-------------
0x00 - Plains (no modifiers)
0x01 - Forest (movement -1, defense +10)
0x02 - Mountains (movement -2, defense +25)
0x03 - Desert (movement -1, defense -5)
0x04 - Swamp (movement -2, defense +15)
0x05 - River (movement -1, defense +5)
0x06 - Coast (trade +20%, defense +8)
0x07 - Highlands (defense +20)
0x08 - Grassland (cavalry +20%)
0x09 - Wasteland (all -10%)
0x0A - Fortress (defense +40)
0x0B - Valley (ambush +30%)
0x0C - Lake (movement -2)
0x0D - Road (movement +1)
0x0E - Bridge (chokepoint)
0x0F - Special Terrain

CITY SIZE MEANINGS
------------------
1 - Small Village (pop < 5,000)
2 - Town (pop 5,000-15,000)
3 - Large Town (pop 15,000-40,000)
4 - City (pop 40,000-100,000)
5 - Major City (pop > 100,000)

PRODUCTION NOTES
----------------
Base gold production per month scales with:
- City size (x 0.5 to x 2.0)
- Terrain (coast/road +20%, wasteland -50%)
- Population (high pop = high taxes)

Base rice production per month scales with:
- Terrain (plains/grassland +30%, desert -50%)
- Season (varies by region)
- Population (workers)

RECRUITMENT MECHANICS
---------------------
Recruitment difficulty (0-99):
- 0-20: Very easy (common soldiers)
- 21-40: Easy (minor generals)
- 41-60: Moderate (skilled generals)
- 61-80: Hard (elite generals)
- 81-99: Very hard (legendary heroes)

Modified by:
- Player charisma
- City loyalty
- Gold offered
- Existing relationships

COMBAT MODIFIERS
----------------
Terrain affects battle outcomes:
- Mountains: Defender +40%
- Forest: Ambush tactics +20%
- Plains: Cavalry +30%
- Desert: Both sides -10% morale
- Swamp: Attacker -30%

GENERAL STAT EFFECTS
--------------------
Leadership (LDR):
- Affects troop morale
- Improves recruiting
- Reduces desertion

Intelligence (INT):
- Strategy success rate
- Diplomacy effectiveness
- Trap detection

Strength (STR):
- Personal combat damage
- Duel victory chance
- Troop damage bonus

Luck (LCK):
- Critical hit chance
- Finding treasures
- Random event outcomes
- Escape success rate

ADVANCED STRATEGIES
-------------------
1. Early game: Focus on recruiting wanderers (0xFF affiliation)
2. Build economy before military expansion
3. Fortress chokepoints control trade routes
4. High INT generals for internal affairs
5. High STR generals for military campaigns
6. Balance loyalty - too high = can't recruit away from enemy

HIDDEN MECHANICS
----------------
- Generals with loyalty < 30 may betray in battle
- Cities with population > 200 produce +50% resources
- Weapon stockpiles improve soldier effectiveness
- Adjacent allied cities share resources
- Trade routes generate passive income
- Seasonal events affect rice production

MODDING TIPS
------------
1. Don't make all generals super-powered (balance!)
2. Create geographical variety (rich/poor regions)
3. Ensure at least one wanderer cluster for recruiting
4. Balance factions - no single mega-power unless intentional
5. Test city connections - broken networks frustrate players
6. Keep some low-stat generals for variety
7. Legendary items should be rare and hard to obtain
